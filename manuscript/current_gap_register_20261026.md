# Current research and article gap register — 2026-10-26

This file is the active handoff context for the manuscript and critical data review. It records unresolved gaps after freezing the top-15 evidence hierarchy.

## Article-production gaps

1. Coverage adjustment is uneven: full technical/recording adjustment exists for the six prespecified keyboard signals; the remaining nine have focused recording-density sensitivity analyses only. Device-change fields are unavailable in some reconstructed cohorts.
2. Robustness and reliability are uneven: leave-one-out, permutation, outlier, test–retest, and alternate-window evidence is concentrated in the six-signal set. Restricted-window, legacy T1, and word-completion rows lack equivalent evidence.
3. Figures are partly complete: the forest plot, top-15 evidence map, and primary-POC scatter/coverage audit now exist; final manuscript placement and any additional supplementary distribution plots remain.
4. Interpretation is drafted but must be integrated into the manuscript Results and Discussion.
5. Prediction reporting needs a separate section with matched mean-baseline comparisons and explicit preprocessing/nesting limitations.

## Scientific and data gaps

6. No independent participant cohort, temporal holdout, or external replication exists.
7. Snapshot-derived IEIs are not direct keystroke timing; word-completion measures have additional continuity, edit-state, paste/prediction, and boundary limitations.
8. Several findings use small cohorts, and some windows were selected after exploratory review.
9. Top-15 q-values do not provide project-wide FDR control; their source or reconstructed testing family must remain visible.
10. Device episodes, app composition, active days, snapshot density, and valid-interval density may still explain part of the patient-level associations. A descriptive primary-POC device/coverage audit is now saved, but the device-unchanged subgroup is only n=6 and cannot validate the signal.
11. Phase 7 10-day extraction remains unintegrated into the article evidence hierarchy.

## New priority from the coverage audit

- In the primary POC patient-level audit (n=28), afternoon IEI change was inversely associated with change in afternoon active days (Spearman ρ=-.489, p=.0082), while 22/28 participants had a device-set change. This does not invalidate the association, but it makes coverage/device composition a central competing explanation that must be shown and discussed before any behavioral interpretation.
